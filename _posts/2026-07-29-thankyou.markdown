## Current Status

So, the UK lawyer has now officially submitted the opposition, and the trademark
is now in _opposed_ status. We don't have any definite dates yet, but essentially
it's a waiting game now.

Hopefully there is more than enough evidence presented to determine a ruling, but
the law is difficult to understand, and nothing is really guaranteed.

## Onwards

The uncertainty is still difficult to deal with at times, but overall we're positive
about the future, and there are many options still available. There are solid improvements
coming in the v1.17.0 release, and many more long-term improvements we are busy with. So, the tech
goes on regardless.

Once again, thank you all for your generosity and your ongoing commitment to
the project.

_The MeshCore Team_
